Instagram accounts get hacked, mistakenly banned, and locked out every single day — and when it happens, the thing people miss most isn't their photos (those are usually recoverable). It's their **network**: the followers and connections they spent years building. Here's how to keep an offline copy so a lost account doesn't mean starting from zero.

## Why back up your followers list

- **Account recovery gone wrong** — if you're locked out or the account is disabled, you at least know who to reconnect with.
- **Starting a new account** — a backup lets you find and re-follow your real connections quickly.
- **Change tracking** — dated backups let you see who unfollowed you over time.
- **Business continuity** — for a brand or creator, the follower relationship *is* the asset.

## What to back up

Two lists cover it: your **followers** and your **following**. Ideally each row keeps the username, full name, and profile URL so the list is still useful months later, even if someone changes their display name.
