Facebook gives you four different ways to get someone out of your life — **Unfollow**, **Restrict**, **Unfriend**, and **Block** — and they do wildly different things. Pick wrong and you either see their posts forever or nuke a relationship you only meant to mute. Here's what each one actually does, from mildest to most final.

## Unfollow: mute them, stay friends

Unfollowing removes someone's posts from your feed. That's all.

- You stay friends; **they see zero difference** — no notification, no visible change anywhere.
- They still see your posts as normal.
- You can still visit their profile whenever you want.

**Use it for:** people you like but whose posting you don't — the aunt who shares twelve memes a day, the friend deep in a MLM phase. It's the zero-risk option: completely undetectable.

## Restrict: they stay friends, but see less

Restrict is the sneaky one. A restricted person stays your friend, but your **friends-only posts are hidden from them** — they only see Public posts and posts you tag them in. Their profile view of you looks normal enough that they typically can't tell.

- No notification.
- Comments they make on your posts can be visible only to them (depending on the surface) — Facebook designed this for softly containing someone without confrontation.

**Use it for:** the coworker or relative you can't unfriend politically, but shouldn't be reading your personal life.

## Unfriend: cut the tie

Unfriending removes the mutual friendship. They drop to stranger-level access: **Public posts only**.

- **No notification is sent** — they'd only notice by visiting your profile and seeing "Add Friend."
- It's mutual: you also stop seeing their friends-only content.
- Reversible only via a new friend request, which they *would* see.
- They can still find your profile and message you.

**Use it for:** people who shouldn't be in your audience at all — strangers, ex-contacts, that guy from a 2012 group project. This is the right tool for a real friends-list cleanup.

## Block: total erasure

Blocking makes you invisible to each other: no profile, no posts, no messages, no search results, no tagging, in both directions. Existing Messenger threads stop working (though history remains until deleted).

- No notification, but blocks are the most *noticeable* — your profile becomes unfindable to them.
- Unblocking has a cooldown before you can re-block.

**Use it for:** harassment, exes you need gone, safety situations. It's the fire alarm, not the volume knob.

## Cheat sheet

- **Their posts annoy you** → Unfollow
- **You don't want them reading your life, but can't cut ties** → Restrict
- **You wouldn't say hi to them in the street** → Unfriend
- **You need them gone entirely** → Block
