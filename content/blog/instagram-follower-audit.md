If your follower count looks healthy but your reach feels dead, it's worth doing a **follower audit** — a structured look at who actually follows you, who never engages, and who you're following into the void. You don't need an analytics subscription for this. You need your lists in a spreadsheet.

## What a follower audit answers

- How many people I follow **don't follow me back**
- Who follows me that I **don't follow back** (my fans)
- How many of my followers are **private**, **verified**, or obviously **inactive/fake**
- Whether my following count is bloated with accounts I forgot about

## Step 1: get your lists out of Instagram

You can't audit what you can't see all at once, and Instagram won't let you export. So the first step is pulling your **followers** and **following** into a spreadsheet.

## Step 2: work the spreadsheet

Once you have the files:

1. **Non-followers:** use the extension's "Not following back" export, or compare the two lists yourself. Decide who's worth keeping.
2. **Fans:** the "Your fans" list is people already invested in you — good candidates to follow back or engage.
3. **Fakes & ghosts:** sort by the private/verified flags and skim usernames. Strings of random letters and numbers, no full name, and default-looking handles are classic bot signatures.
4. **Bloat:** filter your following list by anything you don't recognize and prune.

## Step 3: act, then re-check

Unfollow or clean up based on what you found, then re-export in a month. Because you have dated spreadsheets, you can literally diff them to see who unfollowed you or who's new.

## Quick answers

- **How often should I audit?** Monthly is plenty for most people; creators watching growth might do it weekly.
- **Do I need to pay for a tool?** Manual scans, history, and one-by-one unfollows are free. CSV and Excel exports are Pro features.
- **Will accounts know I audited them?** No. Reading and exporting your lists notifies no one.
- **Can I automate the cleanup?** The extractor exports and analyzes; actually unfollowing is something you do in Instagram itself.
