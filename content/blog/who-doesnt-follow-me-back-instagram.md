"Who doesn't follow me back?" is a common Instagram account-management question, but the app has no dedicated comparison view. Here's how to find out and why you should be careful about *how* you do it.

## The warning first: don't use password apps

Search "who unfollowed me" and you'll find apps and websites that ask you to **enter your Instagram password directly into their service**. Avoid that. Prefer Instagram's own login surface and tools that use the browser session you already control; never give an unrelated site your credentials.

## The logic behind it

"Not following back" is just set subtraction:

> **People you follow** − **People who follow you** = accounts that don't follow you back

So to compute it, you need two lists: your **following** and your **followers**. Compare them by account, and whoever's in "following" but not in "followers" is a non-follower. (Flip it around — followers minus following — and you get your **fans**: people who follow you that you don't follow back.)

Doing this by hand means scrolling both lists, which Instagram deliberately makes tedious and caps for large accounts. That's why you want a tool that collects both lists and does the comparison for you.
