Instagram lets you watch Reels endlessly but gives you no button to save one. The workarounds people reach for — screen recording, or pasting a link into a random "reel downloader" website — either wreck the quality or stamp a watermark across the video (and often ask you to log in, which you should never do). Here's how to get a clean, full-quality MP4 instead.

## Why screen recording isn't good enough

A screen recording re-encodes the video through your display: you lose sharpness, you capture at your screen's frame rate rather than the original, and you pick up your phone's UI, notifications, and audio glitches. For a Reel you actually want to keep or repost, it looks obviously second-hand. Downloading the original file avoids all of that because you're saving the exact MP4 Instagram served.

## Method 1: Chrome DevTools (free, manual)

You can pull the file by hand:

1. Open the Reel in Chrome and press **F12** → **Network** tab.
2. Type `.mp4` into the filter box.
3. Let the Reel play for a second — an entry appears (served from `cdninstagram.com` or `fbcdn.net`).
4. Click it → **Open in new tab** → right-click the video → **Save video as…**

This works and gives you the real file. The catch: Reels are often delivered in separate video and audio streams, or in chunked ranges, so you sometimes get a silent clip or a partial file — and you repeat the whole dance for every Reel.
